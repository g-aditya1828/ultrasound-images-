⑤ ComboLoss — Why Three Terms?
① Weighted BCE
Standard binary cross-entropy with a pos_weight = √((1−fg)/fg) capped at 8×.

Problem it solves: fascicles occupy <1% of image pixels — without upweighting, the model learns to predict all-background and still gets 99% accuracy.
② Focal-Tversky
Tversky index with α=0.3, β=0.7 (heavy recall bias), raised to power 0.75.

Problem it solves: Plain Dice is symmetric between precision and recall. Here we penalise false negatives (missed fascicles) more than false positives.
③ clDice (fascicle only)
Differentiable loss on the soft skeleton of pred & GT masks. Computed via iterative erosion/dilation (5 iters).

Problem it solves: BCE+Tversky rewards spatial overlap but doesn't care if predictions are fragmented. clDice rewards connected, continuous lines.
